# Pilot Location Analysis with Power BI

*Singapore Polytechnic · Persuasive Communication with Data Storytelling · Team project*

## Overview

Our team analysed a sample of 200 Singapore residents to recommend a pilot location for Catch & Go, a location-based mobile game.

## Business Question

Where in Singapore should Catch & Go pilot its new feature to reach its target audience and make participation convenient?

## Project Workflow

- Compared weekly gaming hours and reasons for playing across age groups in Power BI.
- Used maps and charts to explore where respondents lived and their proximity to MRT stations and public Wi-Fi.
- Compared the North-East and West clusters based on audience concentration and accessibility.

## Key Findings

- Respondents aged 50 and above reported the highest average weekly gaming hours in the sample.
- Respondents in this age group clustered in the North-East and West.
- Our team recommended a pilot across connected parks and public spaces in North-East Singapore, citing MRT and LRT connectivity and walkable routes.
